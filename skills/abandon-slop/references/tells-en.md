## Repair Recipes

- Delete throat-clearing and start with the claim.
- Replace abstract verbs with an actor, action, object, and measurable result.
- Replace unsupported superlatives with evidence or remove them.
- Flatten “not X but Y” into the actual assertion.
- Keep list scaffolding only when order carries meaning.
